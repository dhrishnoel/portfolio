~/about

About Me

I’m currently pursuing an MSc in Data Science at Christ University, Bangalore (2026–2028), after completing a BSc in Physics at St. Albert’s College, MG University, Kerala. Physics gave me a grounding in quantitative reasoning and statistical thinking, which is what first pulled me toward data science — I wanted to apply that same rigor to messier, real-world questions.

My technical interests sit at the intersection of statistics, time series analysis and machine learning, and I’m increasingly drawn to applying these to financial and market data — from volatility modeling to sentiment analysis on financial news. Outside of modeling, I also write about what I’m learning, which keeps me honest about actually understanding a concept rather than just using it.

My goal is to grow into a Financial Data Analyst or Research Analyst role at a fintech startup, working on problems in spaces like neobanking, payments, lending or insurtech.
